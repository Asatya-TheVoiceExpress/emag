In Defense of the ‘White SJW’

Let us concede, for a moment, that all such characters are indeed, performative. All of them insincere and cynical actors for whom the only thing that matters is the supposed image of themselves they are trying to project. Let us hold all of this as manifestly true. Does it matter?

You know her, you’ve seen her on your screen, you’ve mocked her and celebrated when she gets her comeuppance—the uppity spoiled brat ever present in the comedies of the tail end of the 2010s. Desperate to project herself as one of the “masses” despite her immense privilege, and overcompensating in her attempts to do so: her naivete and her insistence on denying it is the meat of the joke you, the audience, as well as the other characters are in on. She (gender neutral, though who are we kidding) is, of course, the White SJW.

The latter half of that term, a pejorised acronym of ‘Social Justice Warrior’, itself has an interesting history, one that makes clear why it isn’t quite possible to ‘degender’ the term, regardless of however much one may pretend to be using it in a context ‘devoid of misogyny’. Originally a neutral self descriptor, it became akin to a slur in the cesspit of x.com (formerly Twitter.com) back in 2011 and gained traction in its negative usage during the infamous Gamergate, mostly through vitriolic usage by 4Chan & 8Chan’s self described incels, as they hounded multiple women out of their platforms, homes, and eventually the public sphere.

The term has of course now come to mean (naturally, female, or at the very least effeminate ) performative progressives whose words, actions, and insistence on political correctness far outweighs their impact. It can, and often is, applied to real people, but is now also a popular sitcom trope: Britta from Community, Jonah from Superstore, and Jacob Hill from Abbott Elementary come to mind.

Let me digress for a moment with a Jewish folktale. A rich man comes to a rabbi and says to him, “In my life, I have not been good enough to gain entry into the Lord’s gates. So please, help me build an orphanage.” The rabbi is, of course, delighted to do so, and makes all arrangements. A year in, the rich man comes back and says, “Rabbi, I realised that since I built this orphanage out of selfish need to go to heaven, it is no longer a good deed, and so I shall stop and do penance instead.” The rabbi hits him with his slipper and yells, “Do you think the orphans care? Build the damn home!”

Does it matter?

There are, because of their actions, still less prejudiced microagressions around; there is still a reduction in slurs thrown about the room when they’re in it—even if it is only to avoid a lecture. The 20 USD given to a homeless man with a song and dance is still 20 USD to him he can buy food with (or, if we stick to the genre’s conventions, “illegal drugs” (weed)) .

You may as well be campaigning against a politician fixing a pothole, while you’re at it, because a camera crew is liable to be present.

The White SJW archetype is not exempt to the rule of archetypes in general: these characters exist as set pieces with which show creators are trying to say something, in accompaniment of other set pieces themselves. More often than not, the trope of the White SJW is not performed in isolation; it exists in opposition to other sitcom tropes, specifically in this case that of the apathetic minority, with whom the archetype forms a silent pair. By contrasting the SJW with the minority character(s)—a la Hermione and the elves—the Western sitcom espouses a strange virtue: that of neutral apathy. When the person facing the alleged hardships, such media ask, doesn’t care, then why do you? Why do you, indeed.

Because that is the worst thing a woman can be, is it not? A loudmouthed, irritating woman often bears the brunt of dislike far greater than that which faces her morally darker counterparts.

This is where we reach the murky depths of fandom and its inherent misogyny, with the words ‘Mary Sue’ spat upon any female character like common venom.

What an absurd critique, ‘annoyance’. That her milquetoast insistence on PC behaviour paired with a skewed judgment of her insincerity and ineffectivity is so bothersome to such a large coterie of people that her kind are most often found raking the bottom of likability rankings and acin g ‘most hated’ results in their respective media homes is just misogyny barely hidden under the skin-thin guise of critique of their ‘Whiteness’.

Just call her a shrew and move on, man.
